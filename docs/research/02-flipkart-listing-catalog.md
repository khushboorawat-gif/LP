## 2. Corrections to the first-pass research

- A handheld exists: family 12 (J36S). The earlier statement "no handheld found" was wrong.
- A mini arcade exists: family 11 (M66). The earlier statement "not found" was wrong.
- There are about 10 TV stick families, mostly M15 variants priced around Rs 1,650 to 1,950, plus a higher tier (Max Plush, GS5 Pro, Spider GS5) at Rs 4,000 to 6,000.

## 3. Risk findings

1. Price anchoring is not credible. The same style of product shows MRP 4,699, 4,999, 5,999 or 6,999 with no pattern, and one 23-emulator listing shows MRP 1,999 at 17 percent off while its twin shows 4,999 at 66 percent off. Do not publish crossed-out prices or "% off" on our store. Show one honest selling price.
2. Platform tags are not credible. Listings carry "2DS, 3DS, DS, PS Vita, PSP, Switch". The product does not run those systems. Do not copy.
3. Game claims are not credible. Titles name God of War, Vice City, Liberty City, Tekken 2/3/5/6, WWE SmackDown, Mario, Contra, Street Fighter and Sonic. These are commercial titles, so there are copyright and trademark issues, and heavy 3D games are unlikely to run well on stick-class hardware. Do not name any game title or use box art on our store. The 20,000 to 30,000 game counts are marketing numbers.
4. "4K" appears in nearly every title. Treat it as maximum HDMI output at best. Native rendering resolution is unknown.
5. Customer satisfaction is mixed to weak. Ratings range from 1.7 to 4.0, with the one large sample (577 ratings) at 3.2. Visible reviews include "Not work proper. Loading show half and hour" (2 stars) and "worst product... biggest regret" (1 star on the handheld). Quality and returns handling will drive our reputation. Plan for testing units before selling and a clear replacement policy.
6. Duplicate numbered listings suggest a seller strategy to game ranking. We should not replicate it.
7. Scarcity tags ("Only few left", "Trending") are Flipkart's. We must not copy them unless backed by our real inventory.

## 4. Decisions this suggests (for owner approval)

1. Launch with about 6 products rather than 12: one entry stick (M15), the 23-emulator stick, Max Plush, GS5 Pro, M66 mini arcade, J36S handheld. Drop duplicate variants.
2. Collections that now have real products: TV Sticks, Mini Arcade, Handheld, Gifts (2-controller models).
3. Position on honest clarity: plain-language setup, what is in the box, what it can and cannot play, real warranty. Competitors on the marketplace rely on inflated discounts and game-count claims.
4. Price points: marketplace selling prices of about Rs 1,650 to 1,950 for entry sticks and Rs 4,000 to 6,000 for the higher tier leave thin margins once ads, shipping and returns are included. The owner must supply landed cost to confirm viability.
