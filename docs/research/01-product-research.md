## 3. Specification conflicts and risks

- "4K HD output" appears as a title claim. No source gives native or internal rendering resolution. Similar generic sticks typically output 4K over HDMI while rendering lower. Use wording like "HDMI output up to 4K [confirm]" and never "4K gaming".
- Game counts vary by listing (20,000, 30,000, 40,000 on near-identical generic products). Treat as unverified marketing numbers. Prefer "preloaded library" wording with a confirmed number only if the owner can show it.
- Emulator counts (15, 23, 53) differ across look-alike products, which suggests shared white-label hardware.
- Platform labels on look-alike listings say PS3, PS4, PS5, PS Vita, 3DS. These are not credible compatibility claims. Never imply the device runs or replaces those consoles.
- Titles mention "code in the box", "game and expansion pack", "PSP". Do not repeat these.
- Some listings show "No returns". Confirm what the owner will actually offer.
- Legal: preloaded commercial ROM libraries raise copyright risk. Confirm with the owner how games are licensed. Do not use game names, logos or box art in marketing.
